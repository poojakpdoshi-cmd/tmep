What if I told you that you could create a professional reel using just your voice, without spending hours editing?

That's exactly what I'm building with my new open-source project called TMEP.

Creating a professional reel takes a lot of time. You need to add captions, design motion graphics, synchronize animations, and create smooth transitions.

But what if all of this could be automated?

That's the goal of TMEP.

Simply upload your voiceover, and TMEP is being designed to analyze your speech, generate synchronized captions, create relevant motion graphics, and combine everything into a complete video.

You can even add your own facecam or create an entire animated reel without showing your face.

And because it's open-source, anyone can explore the code, contribute, and help improve it.

TMEP is still under development, and I'll be sharing its real progress, challenges, and results.

Follow along to see what we build!
